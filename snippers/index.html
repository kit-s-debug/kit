03Colour correction

From box dye to blonde, gradually.

Dark home dye doesn’t jump straight to blonde. It lifts through warm stages first. Taking it slowly, and toning at the end, keeps the hair in good condition.

  1. 01Box dyeBlack or very dark, built up at home.
  2. 02First liftDark colour gives way to deep red.
  3. 03Warm stageOrange to gold. Patience matters here.
  4. 04TonedWarmth softened to a natural blonde.
