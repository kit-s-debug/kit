First kit?

Three things
we'll ask you

With this many kits on the wall, it helps to ask. Here's what we'll talk through at the counter.

  1. How much you vape

    A light vaper and a heavy one want different kits. That decides between something like a Pixl or IVG and a bigger Hayati.

  2. The right strength

    Nic salts come in 10mg and 20mg. If you're switching from cigarettes, we'll help you pick a starting point.

  3. Flavours you like

    Fruit, ice, menthol or tobacco. Tell us what you usually go for and we'll point you to the right shelf.
