Before you come in

Good to know

Challenge 25

It's illegal to sell vaping products to anyone under 18. If you look under 25, we'll ask for photo ID: a passport, driving licence or PASS card.

Recycling

Vapes contain lithium batteries and don't belong in the bin. Bring in any used vape, pod or battery, any brand, and we'll recycle it.

Bring your kit

Leaking, burnt taste, not charging? Bring it in. We'll take a look and fit a fresh pod or coil if that's all it needs.

Questions

Asked at
the counter

Do you sell disposable vapes?

No. Single-use vapes have been banned from sale in the UK since 1 June 2025. Everything we sell is rechargeable and refillable, and a pod kit costs less to run within a few weeks.

Can I order online for delivery?

Not at the moment. Add products to a collection list on the shop page and send it to us, or show it at the counter. You pay when you collect, and we'll check ID if needed.

What's the difference between nic salts and shortfills?

Nic salts are 10ml bottles with nicotine already in, made for pod kits and pens. Shortfills are 50ml nicotine-free bottles with room to add a nicotine shot, made for sub-ohm tanks like the 40W Starter Mod Kit.

How often should I change my pod or coil?

Usually every one to two weeks, or as soon as the flavour fades or tastes burnt. Sweeter liquids wear them out faster.

Can you set up my new kit?

Yes, every kit we sell. We'll fill it, prime the coil and show you how to charge it safely, so it's ready to go before you leave.
