Good to know

Challenge 25

It's illegal to sell vaping products to anyone under 18. If you look under 25, we'll ask for photo ID: a passport, driving licence or PASS card.

Recycling

Vapes contain lithium batteries and don't belong in the bin. Bring in any used vape, pod or battery, any brand, and we'll recycle it.

Multi-buy offers

Lost Mary, IVG and Pixl kits are 2 for £12, SKE Crystal bars 4 for £18, and nic salts 4 for £10. Offers are on the shelf labels and applied at the till.

FAQs

Do you still sell disposables?

Single-use vapes have been banned from sale in the UK since 1 June 2025. The kits on our shelves are the rechargeable pod versions, from brands like Lost Mary, IVG, Pixl, Hayati and Elux.

Do you deliver?

Yes, within 10 miles of the shop. Place your order on the order page and choose delivery. We'll email you the delivery charge to confirm, and you pay the driver by cash or card. The driver checks photo ID on every delivery.

What are nic salts?

Nic salts are 10ml e-liquids made for pod kits, in 10mg or 20mg strengths. Ours are £3 each or any four for £10.

Do you sell nicotine pouches?

Yes. We stock Velo, Pablo, Killa and others in a range of strengths. Pouches are for over-18s only, and some are very strong, so ask us before trying one for the first time.

What should I do with my old vapes?

Bring them in. Vapes contain lithium batteries and shouldn't go in household bins. We'll take any brand for recycling.
