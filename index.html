Good to know

Frequently asked

01Do I need to book a table in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome on both floors, but tables and VIP booths are limited. Booking ahead through Private Hire or a VIP Night enquiry guarantees your spot.

02What's the age policy?

Eddie's is strictly 18+. Valid photo ID is required for entry: a passport, driving licence, or PASS card.

03Is there a dress code?

No. There's no dress code on either floor, come as you are.

04Can I hire the whole venue?

Yes, either floor, or the whole building, for private parties, showcases and corporate bookings. Get in touch with your date and numbers to get a package together.

05Where do I park?

There's public parking within a short walk of Quay Street. Get in touch if you'd like directions for the night.
