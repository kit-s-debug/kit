Good to know

Frequently asked

01What are your opening days and hours?

Wednesday, Friday and Saturday. Wednesday and Friday we're open 9pm till 2am, and Saturday we run 9pm till late.

02Is there an entry fee?

Wednesday and Friday are free entry. Saturday's entry fee varies depending on the night, get in touch if you want the price ahead of time.

03Do I need to book a table in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome on both floors, but tables and booths are limited. Get in touch ahead of time and we'll hold one for you.

04What's the age policy?

Eddie's is strictly 18+. Valid photo ID is required for entry: a passport, driving licence, or PASS card.

05Is there a dress code?

No. There's no dress code on either floor, come as you are.

06Can I hire the whole venue?

Yes, either floor or the whole building, for private parties, showcases and corporate bookings. Get in touch with your date and numbers to get a package together.

07Where do I park?

There's public parking within a short walk of Quay Street. Get in touch if you'd like directions for the night.
