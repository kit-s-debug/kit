Teak & woodwork

Craftsmanship in every plank.

Teak and varnished wood give a boat its character, and they need the right care to stay that way. We clean grey, weathered decks back to warm teak, restore and refinish woodwork, and varnish brightwork to a deep, protective shine.

  • Teak deck cleaning & restoration
  • Marine woodworking & repairs
  • Varnishing & brightwork
  • Seams and caulking kept tidy
