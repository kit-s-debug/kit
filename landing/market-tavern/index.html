The story

Two years of work turned a crumbling Victorian storehouse into somewhere Haverfordwest can gather again.

  • The buildingTucked behind the Core of the Poodle record shop on historic Market Street, a stone's throw from the Palace Cinema.
  • The restorationA two-year project led by Malcolm and Cheryl Herbert, part of their Core of the Poodle venture.
  • Doors openThe Market Tavern opened in September 2025: craft beer, wood-fired pizza and a real community room.
