Walk the aisles

The shop is small, but it carries a big range, and almost every wall is spices. Here is what you'll find on the way round.

Whole spices & seeds

A full wall of whole spices: cumin, fennel, nigella, mustard seed (black, brown and yellow), cloves, mace, nutmeg, cardamom (green and black), star anise, ajwain, dill seed and more.

Ground spices & masalas

Big 400g bags of Kashmiri chilli, haldi, coriander, garam masala, tandoori and Madras blends, alongside our own Spice Box curry mix.

Fresh veg & herbs

Coriander, methi, curry leaves, okra, karela, long aubergines, mooli, gourds, finger chillies and scotch bonnets.

Tropical fruit

Thai guava, papaya, elaichi bananas, fresh figs and mangoes in season. Plantain comes both green and ripe.

Roots & tubers

Yam, eddoes, sweet potato and pumpkin, plus loose garlic, ginger and onions sold by weight.

Herbs & Middle Eastern

Bodrum and Sofra herbs, za'atar, sumac, hibiscus, pine nuts and green peppercorns, all on the stand by the door.

Oriental foods

Gochujang, ssamjang, kimchi, Thai curry pastes, bamboo shoots, bean curd, pickled ginger, rice cakes and Manora prawn crackers.

Poppadoms

Plain, cumin, red and green chilli, and garlic poppadoms, with Madras poppadoms in 200g and 250g packs.

Caribbean & African

Dunn's River and Grace seasonings for jerk, oxtail and curry goat, Tropical Sun, suya pepper and baking powders.
