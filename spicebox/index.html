The Spice Box is a spice and world-food shop in the middle of Haverfordwest. We stock whole and ground spices by the bag, fresh chillies, curry leaves, okra and coriander, plus tropical fruit, yams, plantain, noodles, sauces and halal meat. We supply shoppers and restaurants alike.

Walk the aisles

Spices cover whole walls, and beyond them are aisles of rice, dals, flours, noodles, sauces, snacks and store-cupboard staples from South Asia, East Asia, Africa and the Caribbean. Here is what you'll find on the way round.

Whole spices & seeds

A full wall of whole spices: cumin, fennel, nigella, mustard seed (black, brown and yellow), cloves, mace, nutmeg, cardamom (green and black), star anise, ajwain, dill seed and more.

Ground spices & masalas

Big 400g bags of Kashmiri chilli, haldi, coriander, garam masala, tandoori and Madras blends, alongside our own Spice Box curry mix.

Fresh veg & herbs

Coriander, methi, curry leaves, okra, karela, long aubergines, mooli, gourds, finger chillies and scotch bonnets.

Tropical fruit

Thai guava, papaya, elaichi bananas, fresh figs and mangoes in season. Plantain comes both green and ripe.

Roots & tubers

Yam, eddoes, sweet potato and pumpkin, plus loose garlic, ginger and onions sold by weight.

Herbs & Middle Eastern

Bodrum and Sofra herbs, za'atar, sumac, hibiscus, pine nuts and green peppercorns, all on the stand by the door.

Oriental foods

Gochujang, ssamjang, kimchi, Thai curry pastes, bamboo shoots, bean curd, pickled ginger, rice cakes and Manora prawn crackers.

Poppadoms

Plain, cumin, red and green chilli, and garlic poppadoms, with Madras poppadoms in 200g and 250g packs.

Caribbean & African

Dunn's River and Grace seasonings for jerk, oxtail and curry goat, Tropical Sun, suya pepper and baking powders.

Noodles

Shin Ramyun, Buldak in every heat, Neoguri, Chapagetti, Indomie and Mama at 20p a packet. Also rice sticks, vermicelli, sweet potato glass noodles, udon and soba.

Sauces & condiments

Kikkoman and Chinese soy sauces, oyster sauce, fish sauce, hoisin, teriyaki, mirin, sesame and chilli oil, sweet chilli, banana ketchup and Datu Puti vinegar.

Pickles, pastes & tins

Patak's pastes and pickles, plus sambal and Laila pickles. Tins include jackfruit, rambutan, lychees, ackee, callaloo, chickpeas and ready-made dals.

Snacks & sweets

Bombay mix, bhujia, sev, cornflake and Kerala mixtures, plantain and cassava chips, Azka toast and rusks, and Choco Pies. There are mochi ice cream and durian in the freezer.

Drinks

Two chilled fridges and more on the shelves. Rubicon, Maaza and Foco, malts (Vita Malt, Maltina, Mighty Malt, Malta Guinness), Coco Mojo, Hata ramune, Jeera soda, Old Jamaica ginger beer, aloe vera, basil seed drinks and energy drinks.

Store cupboard

Roti, parathas, tortillas and giant naan, eggs, pure butter ghee and vegetable ghee, palm oil, cooking oils and tinned tomatoes.

Dried fish

Shutki for Bengali cooking: Bombay duck (loitta), dried anchovy, chingri, keski and prawns, plus whole crayfish for West African soups.

Rice by the sack

Basmati from Tilda, East End, India Gate, Zeeba and Laila, 1121 sella, Thai jasmine and glutinous rice, sona masoori, Kerala matta, Egyptian, sushi rice and jollof rice, in bags up to 10kg.

Lentils, dals & beans

Chana, toor, moong, urid and masoor dal, green and brown lentils, moth, kala chana, rosecoco, black-eye, kidney, butter and black turtle beans.

Shan & Laziza mixes

The whole range of Shan mixes: biryanis, nihari, haleem, korma, seekh kabab, chicken tikka and chaat masala. There are also Laziza, MDH and Eastern mixes, plus falooda and kulfi mixes.

Flours & grains

Chapatti flour and chakki atta, maida, rice, juwar, bajri and makki flour, powa, semolina, corn meal, idli rava, bulgur, couscous and puffed rice.

Nuts & dried fruit

Almonds (whole, flaked and ground), cashews, pistachios, red and pink peanuts, roasted chana, sultanas, figs, dates and baklava.

African staples

Yellow, white and Ijebu gari, cassava flour, Semovita, pounded yam, ground rice, Golden Morn, palm oil, shito and ackee.

Middle Eastern & Polish

Bodrum and Sofra pickled peppers, olives and tahini, falafel and basbousa mixes, halawa, Polish sauerkraut and gherkins, and PAN maize flour.

Halal freezers

Halal meat, fish and frozen foods

Our chest freezers hold halal meat portioned and labelled in the shop, as well as whole fish and frozen foods from Asia, Africa and the Caribbean.
