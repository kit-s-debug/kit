Our Story

A small shop front with a big world behind it.

From the street, The Spice Box is a green sign and a bay window on Dew Street. Inside, the shelves run floor to ceiling. Whole spices and seeds sit in neat rows, sacks of rice are stacked by the window, and noodles and sauces fill whole aisles. Fresh produce arrives by the box.

It's a shop for cooks. Some come in for a single ingredient a recipe asks for, and some come in for the flavours of home. Plenty come in just to see what's on the shelves. We serve home cooks and trade customers alike.

Over the door: "The Spice Box Food Store · Retailers & Wholesalers · Spices and Oriental Foods"

      Why visit

      Come for one ingredient. Leave with dinner plans.

      1. Many cuisines, one shop

        South Asian, East and South-East Asian, African, Caribbean, Middle Eastern and Polish groceries share the same aisles.

      2. Spices by the bag

        Whole and ground spices in everyday packs and big 400g bags, so you can buy for a single dish or for a busy kitchen.

      3. Fresh ingredients, by the box

        Curry leaves, methi, okra, karela, scotch bonnets, plantain and yam: the fresh things the recipe actually asks for.

      4. Retail & wholesale

        Shopping for a restaurant, a takeaway or a big family event? We sell to trade customers as well as home cooks. Ask in the shop.
