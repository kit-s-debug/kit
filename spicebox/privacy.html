Privacy

Your privacy

This website is an information page for The Spice Box, 27 Dew Street, Haverfordwest SA61 1ST. It has no forms, no accounts and no online ordering. It also has no analytics or advertising trackers.

What the site stores

Nothing. The site doesn't set cookies or keep any information about you. The shelf search runs entirely in your browser.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request.
  • Google Maps only loads if you press "Show the real map" or follow a directions link. Google may then set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy.
  • The links to Facebook, Cylex and the Food Standards Agency take you to those websites, which have their own privacy policies.
