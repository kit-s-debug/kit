Our story

A small salon on Dew Street with a lot on the menu.

Chrysalis comes from the Greek for gold. It names the quiet stage before something new, and it suits a room where nothing is rushed and the small details get proper attention. Brows, lashes, skin, nails, makeup, waxing and tanning, all under one roof in the middle of Haverfordwest.

  • 20+ years in the beauty industry for the salon's lead therapist
  • L4 Level 4 advanced beauty therapist qualification
  • 3× third-place finishes at the UK Hair & Beauty Awards: Best for Brows, Beauty Therapist of the Year and Best Salon Décor
