IForeword

Fun, local, friendly. The shop’s words, and hard to improve on.

Step in from Bridge Street and you are in a family-run independent bookshop: new fiction and non-fiction, history, biography, gardening, travel and natural history, with a particular weakness for two things. Books signed by their authors, and books about this corner of Wales.

Many of those signed and local titles are never listed online at all. They are on the shelves, and the people behind the counter know where.

Around 1891

Paper, ink and the morning news

The shop has served Haverfordwest, in one form or another, since about 1891. It began life as stationers and newsagents, with greeting cards, confectionery and tobacco on the counter beside the papers.

5

1 May 1981

Marley Davies buys a newsagent’s

The shop he takes on stands at 5 Victoria Place, one of the terraces finished in 1839 and named for the young Queen. It trades very much as a newsagent, with very few books. Marley sets about expanding the book department.

7

1996

Down the hill to Bridge Street

The shop moves to 6 Bridge Street, the street that runs down to the Old Bridge. For centuries this was part of the main way through town, carrying trade to and from the Quay.

9

2010

A book for the town

For Haverfordwest’s 900th anniversary celebrations, the Civic Society publishes Mark Muller’s People Who Shaped Haverfordwest. Victoria Bookshop becomes its main outlet, and Marley passes every penny of the sales on to the Society.

11

May 2021

Bookshop of the Month

LoveReading features Victoria Bookshop as its Bookshop of the Month, talking to Marley and to Bethan, who buys the books. The well-attended author events get a mention, and so does the shop’s own summary: fun, local, friendly.

13

Today

Still family-run, still turning pages

Bethan buys the books. The shelves lean towards signed editions and local stories, and new books of every kind fill the rest. If it is not on the shelf, ask at the counter.

VPembrokeshire, on paper

Books about the place you are standing inPembrokeshire: Pembrokeshire

Haverfordwest is the county town of Pembrokeshire, and the shop keeps its shelves that way. Local history, local authors, and the books that only a bookshop in this town would think to stock.

Visiting authors from the area sign here too, so the local shelf and the signed shelf often meet in the middle.
