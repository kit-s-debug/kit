Bridge Street's sandwich counter

Samuel's is a family-run sandwich shop in the middle of Haverfordwest. You say what you want, they make it to order, and it comes back properly filled.

The hot baguettes are what people talk about first. Close behind: the subs, the soup, and the friendly, chatty faces behind the counter.
